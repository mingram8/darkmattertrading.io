What we do

Infrastructure first. Then research. Then risk.

01 · Data

Market-data infrastructure

Low-latency collectors with precise timestamps that record full order books and trade tape across venues, so research runs on what actually happened, not on summaries.

02 · Research

Systematic research

Every idea is written down and judged against criteria set before the test. Most don't survive, and the ones that do get gated to the conditions where they work.

03 · Trading

Proprietary trading

We trade our own capital in digital-asset markets with automated systems, hard risk rules and stops that are tested to survive failure.

How we work

Survive first. Earn second.

Failure-first design

Every system is built by asking how it breaks. Safeguards live where nobody, including us, can quietly switch them off.

Measured, not guessed

Claims need data, and verdicts need pre-registered tests. A good story isn't evidence.

Gate it, don't kill it

When a working idea blows up, we look for what made that situation different before we throw the idea out.

Contact

We run a small number of academic research internships each term, working on live infrastructure under direct mentorship. If you build systems and like hard problems, write to us. For anything else, reach out directly.
